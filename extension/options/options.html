Trimmed page content goes only to your local endpoint. Input fields, passwords and editable areas are excluded. No page history is saved.

How it works & limitations ＋

When protection is on, regular HTTP / HTTPS pages are analysed. Dynamic content and navigation trigger fresh scans, spaced at least 5 seconds apart. Unchanged content is skipped and background tabs pause scanning.

Without an API, or when the AI is unavailable, a local price check looks for amounts such as £1,299 or GBP 1299. It skips some obvious old prices and instalments but cannot reliably identify what an amount refers to. AI is retried after 30 seconds on visible pages.

Only GBP is compared; no exchange rates are guessed. Each request includes up to 24,000 characters of simplified HTML and 16,000 of text. Prices in images, frames, shadow roots or truncated content may be missed. AI and local checks can make mistakes; check the actual shop price.

The same product and price trigger one reminder per page visit. Refreshing, changing rules or scanning manually resets reminders. This is a voluntary reminder, not a payment block or enforced cooling-off period.
